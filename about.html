About Me

Hello everyone, my name is Matt Louis Pacia. I am a 19 year-old Filipino student working for my A.A.S in Business Administration: Computer Applications emphasis. Nice to meet all of you, and maraming salamat po (thank you) for your time!

My Hobbies

As a person who grew up with the Internet and knows a good amount of technology, I enjoy singing, playing video games, and watching different animes. But of course, I do also have a passion for art, especially drawing as a full-time hobby.

Creative Interests

If you get to know me, then you'll realize how much I can gush about almost anything I like. For instance, some of my favorite video games include Fortnite, a plethora of the Super Mario games, Mortal Kombat 11, Team Fortress 2, and Resident Evil 4.

That's already a good example of my hyperfixations. Another thing about me is that I love anything sci-fi and fantasy, as they draw me into creative worlds in all sorts of media. Whether it's intergalactic battles to spirtual powers of ancient Japanese gods, I'm always invested in what these genres can offer through storytelling and especially art.

Current Aspirations

My goals in life are to be a professional in computer technology and improve as an aspiring artist. Both are difficult paths in their own right, and I'm certain that I will face many challenges. However, as long as I keep persevering and working hard, it's definitely possible to reach my dreams.

There Is Always Room to Improve

This is only one part of my journey. There is still so much to learn, create, and discover.
